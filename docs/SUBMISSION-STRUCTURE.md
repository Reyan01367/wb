## 3. Weekly Process PDF

Landscape A4, same fonts and colours as the website (Inter Tight, Instrument Serif, JetBrains Mono; `#0E0E0C`, `#EFEBE3`, `#FF4B1F`).

1. Cover sheet (mandatory Regent's form)
2. Contents
3. Introduction: aims, why healthcare, career link (½ page)
4. **Weekly log**: one spread per week, always with the same 5 headings:
   - **Goal this week**
   - **What I did** (screenshots: research boards, sketches, contact sheets, Figma Weave prompts + outputs, Adobe working files)
   - **Decisions + why** (cite sources in-text: *(Michie, van Stralen and West, 2011)*)
   - **Feedback received** (tutor/peer) → **what I changed**
   - **Reflection** using *What? → So what? → Now what?* (Rolfe, Freshwater and Jasper, 2001)
5. Suggested week plan:

| Week | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Brief analysis, personal positioning (health), project selection |
| 2 | Research P1 (Bupa): market, audience, trend + competitor audit |
| 3 | Research P2 (NHS): COM-B diagnosis, behaviour change cases (This Girl Can, Dumb Ways to Die, Lifebuoy) |
| 4 | Research P3 (NIVEA): FMCG category, skinification trend |
| 5 | Insight + concept development, all three (show rejected routes!) |
| 6 | Photo shoot P1: shot list, contact sheets, selects, retouching |
| 7 | Figma Weave P3: prompt iterations (show bad generations too) |
| 8 | Adobe production P2 + layouts for all three |
| 9 | Website build + intro film script/shoot |
| 10 | Career pack: CV, SWOT, LinkedIn, job role, cover letter |
| 11 | Formative feedback → changes made (before/after screenshots) |

6. Final reflection (1 page): learning across the module, and how it informs healthcare management
7. References (Harvard)
8. Mandatory AI Log

> Markers reward **iteration**. Include rejected concepts, failed AI outputs and before/after edits. A tidy book with no mess reads as "documented the final", not "documented the process".

## 4. Career Pack PDF

Same visual identity as the website, so the rubric's "all elements work together as a distinctive personal brand" applies (Band 6).

1. Cover sheet (mandatory)
2. **Personal brand statement**: one line ("Healthier behaviour, by design.") + 80-word positioning
3. **Identified job role**: screenshot of a real listing + a table mapping each requirement to your evidence. Bridge roles that suit an advertising-to-healthcare-management path:
   - Health communications / campaigns officer (NHS trust, ICB, UKHSA, charity)
   - Junior strategist at a health agency (e.g. healthcare/pharma comms networks)
   - NHS Graduate Management Training Scheme (strategic long-term target)
4. **CV**: 1 page, branded, tailored to that role
5. **Cover letter**: for that exact role, referencing a portfolio project as proof
6. **Personal SWOT**: 2×2 grid, plus an **action** for each W and T (that turns a SWOT into a development plan, MLO 03)
7. **LinkedIn**: screenshots of headline, About, featured section (the 3 campaigns + website), plus a 4-post content plan
8. Professional development plan: 1 / 3 / 5 years (MLO 03)
9. References + AI Log
